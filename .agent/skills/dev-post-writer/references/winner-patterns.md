# What the Weekend challenge winners did (study, do not copy)

Source: the winners post and the Gemma category winner write-up (links in `docs/POST_OUTLINE.md`).

- Specific, numeric titles: counts of test cases, real vs synthetic, a named failure.
- One real person and a concrete situation, not "users".
- A held-out test set, real documents beside synthetic ones, and head-to-head runs of several model sizes with timings.
- Design where **code owns the dangerous parts** and the model reads and explains. The failure ("calm and wrong") is described, then engineered out, then tested.
- A numbered "why open matters" section, plus a paragraph acknowledging a closed alternative exists.
- Model-free tests in CI so reviewers can trust the claims, and an eval document in the repo.
- A short demo video, a repo link, and a clear partner-category paragraph.
- Reaction counts were low on some winners: the judging looked at the write-up and the engineering, not popularity.
